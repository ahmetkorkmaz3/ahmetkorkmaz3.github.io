About

I am a full stack developer with more than five years of experience. I build web applications and REST APIs with Laravel and Symfony on the backend, and React, Next.js and Vue on the frontend.

At Moneo, I currently work on the Karaca e-commerce platform. I redesigned its payment flows, and I help the team make decisions on critical issues. Before that, I worked on Kitapyurdu and ConectoHub.

I think of software as a craft. I care about clean code, automated tests, SOLID principles and domain-driven design. I studied Software Engineering at Karadeniz Technical University, where I led the university Software Club.
